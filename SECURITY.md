# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in AORBIT, in Doblier infrastructure, or in this documentation site, please email:

**security@doblier.io**

Include a description, reproduction steps, and impact assessment if possible. Please do **not** open a public GitHub issue for security reports.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement within 2 business days.
- Triage and severity assessment within 5 business days.
- We will keep you informed of remediation progress and coordinate disclosure timing with you.

## Safe harbor

Good-faith security research conducted in accordance with this policy will not be the subject of legal action by Doblier Inc. Do not access customer data, degrade service availability, or test systems you are not authorized to test.

The full disclosure policy is published at: https://docs.doblier.io/platform/security/vulnerability-disclosure
